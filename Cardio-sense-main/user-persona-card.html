Thomas Miller

Hypertension Patient

Age 68 years old
Occupation Retired Teacher
Tech Literacy Basic (Smartphone user)
Medical Focus Continuous BP Monitoring
"I know I need to track my blood pressure daily, but using paper diaries feels outdated, and I never know if the numbers I'm seeing are actually dangerous until my next doctor's visit weeks later."

Goals & Needs

  • To have a system that automatically logs and visualizes blood pressure without relying on pen and paper.
  • To receive immediate, understandable alerts if a physiological reading crosses into a dangerous threshold.
  • To effortlessly share medical history with his cardiologist securely before an appointment.

Pain Points & Challenges

  • Traditional medical portals are clunky, requiring too many clicks and complicated password setups.
  • He experiences "Snap-shot Flaw"—his doctor only sees his health condition on the exact day of his visit.
  • Struggles to interpret raw medical data (e.g., 145/95) and lacks an ecosystem to advise him proactively.
